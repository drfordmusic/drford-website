Privacy

Plain version: I collect only what you send me, I use it to talk with you about your music, and I never sell it.

Effective November 13, 2026

Who I am

This site belongs to Daniel Robert Ford, doing business as Dr. Ford Productions in Nashville, Tennessee. I’m responsible for the information you share here. For any privacy question or request, email daniel@drford.com.

What I collect

  • What you type into a form: your name, email, phone number (optional), your message, your answers about the song and any link to a demo you share.
  • Where you came from: if you arrive through one of my ads, the form notes which ad (for example, “Instagram”) so I know which ads work. It never identifies you across other websites.
  • Basic server logs: like every website, the server records your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for, the time and the page that linked you here. I use these to keep the site running and secure, and to count visits from each ad.

The site has no cookies, no analytics service, no advertising pixels and no third-party scripts.

What’s stored in your browser

If you arrive through one of my ads, your browser saves a short note naming that ad, so the form can include it if you contact me later. It holds no personal information, stays on your device and is ignored after 90 days. Clearing your browser’s site data deletes it.

Why I use it

  • To answer your inquiry and, if we work together, to make your record. You ask me to; that’s the basis.
  • To keep the site secure and see which ads bring people here. That’s my legitimate interest in running a small business, and it’s kept to the minimum.

Who else handles it

  • Formspree delivers form messages to my email. It processes them on my behalf in the United States.
  • My web host stores the site and its server logs.
  • PayPal, only if you buy a gift certificate through a payment link I send you. PayPal’s own privacy policy covers that payment.

I never sell your information, and I never share it for advertising. Links to Instagram, Facebook and YouTube take you to those sites, and their policies apply there.

How long I keep it

  • Inquiries: up to two years, so I can pick up the conversation if you come back.
  • If you become a client: as long as the project needs, plus what tax and business records require.
  • Server logs: up to 90 days.

Your choices and rights

You can ask me for a copy of your information, ask me to correct it or delete it, or tell me to stop using it. Email daniel@drford.com and I’ll answer within 30 days. Nothing changes in how I treat you for asking.

These rights apply wherever you live, including California, the EU and the UK. If you’re in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Do Not Track: this site doesn’t track you across other websites, so there’s nothing to switch off.

Young artists

This site isn’t meant for children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect their information. If you’re under 18, have a parent or guardian reach out with you.

Security

The site runs on an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and forms are sent securely. No method is perfect, so please don’t send passwords or payment details through a form.

AI training

The AI assistants from OpenAI, Anthropic and Apple may learn from this site’s text. They may not collect its audio or photos, and other AI training crawlers are blocked. Search engines and AI answer tools can find every page.

Changes

If this policy changes, I’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
